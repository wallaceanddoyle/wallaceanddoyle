We build Tools for Fishing.

Welcome to Wallace & Doyle, home of the Vermont Fishing Rod Works. Based in South Hero, Vermont, we handcraft fishing rods, nets, and fly boxes. Everything we build is meant to be worked hard and made to last. Serious tools for serious anglers.

Rods

Custom built, since 1984.

Since 1984, Wallace & Doyle has been building custom crafted carbon fiber and fiberglass fishing rods. Each rod is made to order, the foundation of the build a blank chosen to suit your specific requirements.

Our focus on quality is blended with creative grip and threadwork that delivers a high-performance rod simply not available elsewhere. We proudly use quality USA-made components from respected manufacturers like Rod Forge, Northfork Composites, St. Croix, REC, Snake Brand, Lemke, and others.

For all your rod needs, Spin, Bait casting, Fly, and Ice, we provide a total solution package.

Nets

Bent-laminated
landing nets.

We start with quality Vermont rough-sawn lumber and fashion bent-laminated landing nets. Featuring our exclusive Dynamic Structural Core technology, each net is crafted with precision-fit laminations, optional hoop and handle sizes to suit your specific needs, and rubber net bags from Cumings Nets. An optional nylon mesh net is also available.

Choose from our ready-to-fish inventory or build your own. Nets are available in four hoop sizes with handles ranging from 8 to 40 inches. Wood options include Maple, Cherry, Black Walnut, Ash, and many others.

Ready for the stream or pond, kayak, canoe, or the boat, let a Wallace & Doyle net enhance your next fishing adventure.

Fly Boxes

Grain-matched
and built to last.

Each fly box is made with Vermont hardwood, carefully selected to feature the beauty of the wood and meticulously grain-matched.

Featuring SOSS barrel hinges and rare-earth magnets, each box provides ample room for flies without excess bulk or weight.

Rod and net building are extensions of woodworking, complementary and mutually skill-building. Our studio provides the perfect environment to create and produce simple treasures, built to be used every day.
